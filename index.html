Our Story

Treats By Tor began in a small home kitchen with a rolling pin, a well-worn French baking textbook, and a belief that pastry made the classical way is worth the extra time. Every croissant is laminated by hand, every tart shell is blind-baked to order, and every custom cake starts with a conversation about what you're celebrating. We bake in small batches, source thoughtfully, and treat each order like it's the only one on the list that day.

FAQ

Q: How far in advance should I order a custom cake?
A: We recommend at least two weeks' notice for custom cakes, and four to six weeks during peak wedding and holiday season. Simpler orders, like a dozen macarons, can often be arranged with less notice.

Q: Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?
A: We offer a small selection of gluten-free and dairy-free items. Please note any allergies in your inquiry — while we take care with substitutions, our kitchen handles gluten, dairy, and nuts, so we can't guarantee a completely allergen-free environment.

Q: Is delivery or pickup available?
A: Pickup is available at the bakery by appointment. Local delivery can be arranged for larger orders and events — just include your address and date when you reach out.

Q: Do you cater weddings and private events?
A: Yes — we love building dessert tables, favors, and centerpiece cakes for weddings and private events of any size. Reach out with your date and guest count and we'll put together a proposal.
